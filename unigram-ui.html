Why Unigram exists

Every platform has a fatal flaw.
We bridge all three.

YouTube
No credibility

Anyone can post anything. You can't tell if a mentor actually knows what they're teaching. No way to verify if a student actually learned.

LinkedIn
No proof of skill

Recommendations are vague, written by people who barely worked with you. Certifications are self-reported. Recruiters can't trust what they see.

Udemy / Coursera
No real relationship

You complete a course, get a badge from a faceless platform. No mentor knows your name. No one vouches for you personally to a recruiter.

Unigram bridges all three gaps
Verified mentors

Mentor-signed certs

Built-in relationship

Student follows mentor, watches content, submits work, gets feedback. The cert comes after a real interaction — not just a quiz.
